Lesson from the Harrison St job in 2025. The mineral fibre ceiling tile was quoted at 6 weeks and
arrived in 14. We ate two weeks of delay and the contractor filed a variation.

What I do now: always get the lead time from the rep in writing before the PO, and always carry one
pre-checked alternate in the spec so a substitution does not restart the whole selection.
