My own preferences for acoustic ceilings. I avoid visible directional texture in client-facing areas.
In open office a standard square lay-in on a 15/16 inch grid is fine; tegular edges only where the
client is paying for the look. Northgate specifically wants a matte white, smooth face on Mission St.
When I substitute a product I match NRC and fire class first, then CAC, then size and edge, then
recycled content. The substitution request always goes to the contractor's project manager with the
datasheet links so they can price it the same day.
