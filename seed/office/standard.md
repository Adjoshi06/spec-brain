Office standard for acoustical panel ceilings (company data, applies to every project).

Open office and open collaboration areas: NRC 0.85 or higher.
Enclosed rooms adjoining open office (meeting rooms, focus rooms): CAC 35 or higher.
Fire performance: Class A per ASTM E84 (flame spread 25 or less, smoke developed 50 or less).
Standard module: 2x2 (24 by 24 inch) lay-in on a 15/16 inch exposed tee grid unless the design says otherwise.
Light reflectance: 0.85 or higher preferred for open office.
Recycled content: report it for every product; 30 percent or more is the target on LEED projects.
Every substitution must be checked against these numbers before it is sent to a contractor.
